About

A festival for the curious

ACROSS is a classical music festival for all curious listeners, whether you are new to classical music or already know it well. The bar stays open, the atmosphere is informal, and the musicians are right there in front of you.

The festival is curated by NOVO Quartet. Every September, at the very end of summer, we invite like-minded ensembles to share the stage and see what happens in the moment.

Venue

LiteraturHaus

Møllegade 7
2200 København N

An intimate hall in Nørrebro, with musicians and audience a few metres apart.
